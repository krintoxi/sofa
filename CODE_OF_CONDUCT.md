# Code of conduct

This covers the repository: issues, pull requests, Discussions and reviews.

- **Talk about the work, not the person.** Disagree as hard as the facts allow, and stay civil doing it.
- **Assume the other person is trying to help.** Most people here are running Sofa at home in their spare time.
- **No harassment, no slurs, no sexual remarks, no threats, and nobody's private details** (addresses, real names
  they didn't give, what's in their library).
- **Keep it on Sofa.** No advertising, and no asking where to find films or channels: Sofa plays what you already
  have, and this isn't the place for the rest.
- **Reports and screenshots:** blank anything that names a person or a title you'd rather not share. A Sofa Box's
  report code already leaves those out.

Something off? Write to hello@edgeventure.uk. We read it, and we may edit or remove a comment, lock a thread or
block an account when that's what it takes. If we got it wrong, tell us at the same address.
