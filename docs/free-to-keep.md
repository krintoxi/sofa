# Free to keep

**Movies → Free to keep** and **Shows → Free to keep** (admins, at home) list films and TV shows that anyone may
download and keep, from the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org). **Save** puts a film in Movies and a show's
episodes in Shows, like any other file: named, with its details, and its poster when Sofa's movie lookup knows it.

## Which films

Only what the Archive marks as free to keep:

| The mark | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Public domain | No one owns it any more: anyone may copy, keep and share it. |
| CC0 | Its maker gave up every right to it. |
| Creative Commons (CC BY, BY-SA, BY-ND, BY-NC...) | Anyone may copy and keep it, as the licence says (NC: not for money; ND: unchanged). |
| No licence, made before 1929, in the Archive's Feature Films | Its copyright in the United States has run out. |

The mark is the word of whoever put the film on the Archive, as it is on the Archive itself. Each film's page says
which mark it has, what it means and whose word it is, and Sofa saves it beside the file (an `.nfo` with the title,
year, the Archive page, the licence and its address).

**Adult films are never here.** The Archive's own adult collection is left out of every search, and so is anything
whose title or subjects say sex or nudity. Adult films free to keep are in the Adult section's own page (see
[adult.md](adult.md)).

## The pages

- **Rows:** a film on top, then what's being saved, most watched, by kind (film noir, horror, westerns, comedy...),
  and by decade. Shows by decade.
- **A film's page:** like a movie's, with its licence in plain words, where it will go, its size, and Save. A real
  poster when Sofa's movie lookup knows the film (its own settings: nothing is looked up with lookups off), else the
  Archive's picture.
- **A show's page:** its episodes, numbered as the Archive names them ("Ep01"), else in the Archive's order. That is
  not always the order they first aired.
- **Saving:** one file at a time; each card and page shows how far it is, then **In Movies** or **In Shows**.

## Who and when

- Admins only, at home or over Tailscale: never visitors, guests, Kids profiles, or away from home over the internet,
  and never on a public Sofa.
- Not with **Work offline** on.
- What Sofa asks the Archive: its search, a film's details and then its file. Lists are kept six hours.

The disc page's **Get it free** (a disc's film, when the Archive has it free) uses the same rules and the same
downloads. It keeps to the marks that allow any use (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), as it always has.
