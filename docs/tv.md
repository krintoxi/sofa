# Watching on a TV

## Fire TV (Silk browser)

1. Open **Silk** on the Fire TV.
2. Go to `http://<the Sofa computer's address>:8090` (the Server page lists it under
   **How to watch**). Sign in.
3. Press **Menu** on the remote and **bookmark** it, so next time it's one click away.

Sofa notices it's on a TV and switches to **TV mode**: bigger buttons, a clear yellow
highlight that follows the remote, and the player fills the screen. (Any other device can
switch TV mode on under **Theme**.)

### The remote

| Button | On the library pages | While watching |
|---|---|---|
| ◀ ▶ ▲ ▼ | move between things | controls hidden: ◀ ▶ skip 10 seconds (with a preview picture); ▲ ▼ show the controls |
| | | on the timeline: ◀ ▶ skip, ▼ goes to the buttons |
| OK (centre) | open / play | pause and play; on **Skip Intro** or **Next episode** it does that |
| Back | go back | closes a panel (episodes, subtitles, options) first, then leaves the player |
| ⏯ Play/Pause | | pause and play |
| ⏪ ⏩ | | back 10 / forward 30 seconds |

When **Skip Intro** appears it's already selected: just press OK. When the credits start,
**Next episode** is selected, and it starts by itself after 10 seconds.

Live TV: with the controls hidden, ▲ ▼ change channel.

### Who's watching?

When Sofa opens on the TV it asks who's watching (if there's more than one profile): pick
yours with the remote. Profiles that can see the Adult section or run the server have a lock:
type their 4-digit PIN with the remote's number keys or the on-screen pad (or their password,
until they set a PIN). Set your color and PIN from **Who's watching? → Your profile**, or by
pressing your name at the top. Switching only works at home, never for guests.

### Tips

- Converted videos (MKV, AVI) take a few seconds to start the first time. Turn on
  **Server → Converting → Convert new files automatically** (on by default) and they'll
  start instantly after that.
- If the picture stutters on an older Fire TV stick, convert the file to MP4 (see
  [playback.md](playback.md)); it then plays without the computer adapting it.

## Smart TVs, Roku, VLC (no browser needed)

With **Server → Settings → Share with TVs** on, Roku Media Player, VLC (on Fire TV, Apple
TV, phones) and most smart TVs list "Sofa" by themselves under media servers. That way has
no sign-in, no Skip Intro and no Adult section; use the browser for the full experience.
